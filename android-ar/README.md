# JH-Safety AR — Android AR Mobile Client

Augmented Reality Vocational Training & Safety Simulator for Industrial Workers in Jharkhand's Mining, Steel, and Mica Sectors.

## Key Features
- **Zero Headset Requirement:** Operates on standard mid-range Android smartphones (Android 10+).
- **Trilingual Localization:** Complete native support for **Hindi (हिंदी)**, **Santali (ᱥᱟᱱᱛᱟᱲᱤ - Ol Chiki)**, and **English**.
- **Interactive AR Modules:**
  1. *Underground Coal Mine Fire & Explosion Safety* (6 practical AR steps).
  2. *Methane (CH4), CO Gas Detection & Confined Space Protocol* (5 practical AR steps).
- **Graceful Fallback:** Automatic 3D Simulation Mode on devices without ARCore hardware.
- **Offline-First Architecture:** Complete offline simulation and assessment with queue-based surface batch synchronization.
- **Dynamic QR Verification:** Generates cryptographic QR credentials verifiable via mobile scanner or web compliance portal.
