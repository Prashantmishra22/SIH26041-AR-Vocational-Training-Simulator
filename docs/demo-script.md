# JH-Safety AR — Hackathon Presentation & Video Demo Script

**Target Duration:** 3 to 5 Minutes  
**Audience:** Smart India Hackathon / Innovation Jury & DGMS Safety Inspectors

---

## Act 1: Problem Statement & Vision (0:00 - 0:45)
- **Visual:** Map of Jharkhand showing Dhanbad, Bokaro, Jamshedpur, and Koderma mining belts.
- **Narrator:** *"Every year, hazardous conditions in underground coal mines, steel melt shops, and mica adits in Jharkhand lead to preventable workplace injuries. Traditional classroom training fails because workers cannot experience life-threatening hazards safely, language barriers exclude tribal miners who speak Santali and Hindi, and external VR headsets are expensive and fragile in dusty underground pits.*
- **Solution Pitch:** *"Introducing **JH-Safety AR**: a mobile Augmented Reality training and safety compliance certification platform that runs on mid-range Android smartphones without any wearable headset."*

---

## Act 2: Trilingual AR Simulation Demo (0:45 - 2:15)
- **Visual:** Screen capture of the Android AR app.
- **Step 1 — Language Selection:** Tap **Santali (Ol Chiki)** or **Hindi**. Show immediate localized UI.
- **Step 2 — Surface Plane Detection:** Point camera at flat surface. Yellow reticle locks onto floor.
- **Step 3 — Coal Mine Fire Module:**
  - *Task 1:* Thermal hotspot scanning (>65°C detection).
  - *Task 2:* Klaxon emergency alarm trigger.
  - *Task 3:* SCSR emergency breathing apparatus donning within 20s.
  - *Task 4:* Extinguisher PASS protocol (Pull, Aim, Squeeze, Sweep).
- **Step 4 — Methane Gas Module:**
  - Demonstrates multi-gas probe zero calibration and telescopic roof cavity testing.

---

## Act 3: Assessment & Dynamic QR Certification (2:15 - 3:15)
- **Visual:** Post-training 10-question safety examination in Hindi/Santali.
- **Scoring Breakdown:** Shows **60% Practical + 40% Theory = 94.4% Score (Passed >= 70%)**.
- **Certificate Generation:** Instant issuance of DGMS-Accredited Digital Certificate with cryptographic hash and dynamic QR code.

---

## Act 4: QR Verification & Admin Compliance Dashboard (3:15 - 4:30)
- **Visual 1 (Mobile Scanner):** Point phone camera at QR code -> Instant green badge: *GENUINE CERTIFICATE VALIDATED*.
- **Visual 2 (Web Admin Dashboard):**
  - Live KPIs: 1,284 Workers, 986 Certified, 88.4% Compliance Rate.
  - Interactive Recharts: Monthly training trajectory, district rankings (Dhanbad 90%, Jamshedpur 90.9%), language breakdown (Hindi 65.9%, Santali 24.3%, English 9.8%).
  - Search & filter worker table.
  - Test live certificate verification modal.

---

## Act 5: Offline Mode & Conclusion (4:30 - 5:00)
- **Visual:** Turn on Airplane Mode -> Show uninterrupted AR training and local SQLite caching -> Reconnect Wi-Fi -> Show automatic batch sync to backend.
- **Closing Statement:** *"JH-Safety AR delivers accessible, life-saving vocational safety training to the grassroots industrial workforce of Jharkhand, combining cutting-edge AR with inclusive vernacular education."*
